Teach me: $ARGUMENTS

Rules for the explanation:
1. **Plain English first** — one or two sentences describing what it is and why it exists, no jargon.
2. **A concrete worked example with real numbers** — walk through it step by step showing the actual arithmetic/values, not just formulas. Small numbers (2–3 digits) so I can follow.
3. **Then name the jargon** — after I've seen the idea, give the technical terms people use for it so I can read about it elsewhere.
4. **One common mistake or misconception** people have with this.
5. Keep it under ~400 words. Prefer a small table or numbered steps over paragraphs.

If the topic depends on my project's code, ground the example in that code rather than a generic case.
